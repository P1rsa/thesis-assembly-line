Why the Thesis Changes Everything
A thesis is the spine of your essay, without it, every other component is rendered worthless. It transforms a loose collection of observations into a single, arguable claim that gives your reader a reason to care.

Every piece of evidence, every literary device, every "so what" moment, every perspective should be directly traceable back to your thesis. It turns a paragraph into a stance, and a stance into an essay. It's not just an introduction line — it is the gravitational centre that holds everything together.
